How Does a Salary Calculator Work?

CTC, gross salary and take-home salary are different. CTC may include employer contributions and benefits that do not arrive in your bank account each month. This calculator provides an estimate based on the assumptions you enter.

CTC vs In-Hand Salary

In-hand salary is generally lower than CTC because of employee PF, income tax and other deductions. Employer-side contributions can also be included in CTC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CTC?

CTC means Cost to Company: the estimated annual cost an employer incurs for an employee.

What is in-hand salary?

It is the amount you actually receive after applicable deductions.

Is CTC the same as take-home salary?

No. CTC can contain components that are not paid directly as monthly cash.
